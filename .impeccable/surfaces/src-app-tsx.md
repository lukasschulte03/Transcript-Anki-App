## Scope
Operate-mode redesign of Lectio's complete Windows desktop surface for students who move from lecture material to reviewed Anki cards.

## Direction contract
THESIS: A quiet study desk where material and the next useful action lead; it refuses a dashboard made from competing cards.

OWN-WORLD: ThemePalette surfaces, fine separators, compact controls, restrained radius, Geist type, Lucide icons, and one clear primary action per view.

STORY: The student sees where they are, what material exists, and what to do next; slides and audio are equal routes, transcript is optional.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Stable navigation and library tree frame one continuous work surface. Lecture identity and adaptive action sit above source tabs; audio stays reachable at the bottom. Details appear on demand.

FORM: Code-led, task-first desktop workspace; user-pinned direction from DESIGN.md, no concept seed required.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
